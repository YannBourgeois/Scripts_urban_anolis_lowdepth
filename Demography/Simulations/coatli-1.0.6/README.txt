Compiling:
    To compile coatli, you should type 'make' in the coatli folder.  

Documentation:
    The latest version of the documentation and examples of usage can be 
    found at http://sourceforge.net/p/coatli/wiki/Home/

Getting Help:
    The best way to get help regarding coatli is to email the mailing
    list: coatli-help@lists.sourceforge.net 
    
Citation:
    If you make use of moment calculations provided by coatli in your research, I would appreciate a 
    citation of one of the two following papers:

    Ferretti, L., Klassmann, A., Raineri, E., Wiehe, T., Ramos-Onsins, S. E., & Achaz, G. (2018). 
    The expected neutral frequency spectrum of linked sites. Theoretical Population Biology, 1–10. 
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tpb.2018.06.001

    Klassmann, A., & Ferretti, L. (2018). 
    The third moments of the site frequency spectrum. Theoretical Population Biology, 120, 16–28. 
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tpb.2017.12.002
